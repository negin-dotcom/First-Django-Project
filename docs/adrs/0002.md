<h1>ADR - 0002: Preserve historical order item prices .<h1>
<h1>status: Accepted - 2026/09/01</h1>


<br>
<h2>Context: <h2>
<p>We have 2 prices, Product.price and OrderItem.price. 

For example,
Day 1: Product.price = 100, Customer creates an order OrderItem.price = 100.
Day 2: Product.price = 120.

So the day 1's orderitem.price must not change.
Product prices can change over time but the historical orders must preserve the price.
</p>


<br>
<h2>Decision: <h2>
<p>We will store the product's current price in orderitem.price when the orderitem is created.</p>


<h2>Consequences:</h2>
<h3>pros:
    <ul>
        <li>Historical orders remain accurate.</li>
    </ul>

cons:
    <ul>
        <li>The price is duplicated in Product and OrderItem.</li>
    </ul>
</h3>
